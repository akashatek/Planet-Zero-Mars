# Planet Zero Mars (PZM)

Inspired by Alone on Mars, about a trip to colonise mars. What if you were the first person to land and create an habitable environement. 

Classic "Rule of 3s" for human survival
 * 3 Seconds — Shelter (Extreme Environments): In immediate hazards—like the vacuum of deep space, falling through thin ice, or being trapped in a burning room—a single bad decision or exposure will render you unconscious or kill you almost instantly.

 * 3 Minutes — Oxygen: Brain damage begins after roughly 3 to 5 minutes without oxygen. Securing clear airways or escaping smoke, drowning, or low-oxygen environments is the immediate physical priority.

 * 3 Hours — Heat / Climate: Uncontrolled heat loss (hypothermia) or extreme heat exposure (hyperthermia) can compromise your cognitive functions and bodily operations within hours. Finding or building shelter and insulating your body comes before seeking food or water.

 * 3 Days — Water: Dehydration rapidly degrades physical strength, decision-making, and kidney function. In mild conditions, 3 days is the average limit without water (though extreme heat shortens this drastically).

 * 3 Weeks — Food: The human body can consume stored glycogen, fat, and muscle mass to sustain baseline metabolic functions for weeks, making food the lowest immediate survival priority in a short-term crisis.
